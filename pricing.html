Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between the Basic and Professional Food Database access?

The **Basic Plan** includes a curated list of ~2000 common Indian and Ayurvedic foods. The **Professional and Clinic Plans** unlock the full **8000+ database**, which includes global foods, rare herbs, and extensive macro/micro and detailed Guna/Virya/Vipaka analysis.

Do you offer a free trial period?

Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial on our **Professional Plan**. You can sign up via the 'Sign Up' button to explore all the features, including the 8000+ database, risk-free.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?

Absolutely. You can change your subscription plan at any time directly from your dashboard's billing section. Upgrades are immediate, and downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is the data secure and HIPAA/GDPR compliant?

VedaNova uses industry-leading encryption and security protocols. We adhere to all major data protection regulations to ensure patient privacy and compliance in your practice.
