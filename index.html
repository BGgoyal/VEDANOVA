Our Philosophy

Fundamental Pillars of Healing

Our software is built on these foundational concepts of Ayurvedic science.

Dincharya (Daily Routine)

The prescribed **daily schedule** for a balanced life. We help plan meals and habits aligned with the patient's biological clock .

Ritucharya (Seasonal Regimen)

Guidance on **diet and lifestyle** adjustments according to the **six Indian seasons** to maintain throughout the year.

Agni (Digestive Fire)

The core **metabolic fire** responsible for digestion and nutrient assimilation. Our plans focus on prescribing that optimizes and strengthens.
